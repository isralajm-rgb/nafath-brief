## Overview
Nafath is a national digital login service concept that aims to provide users with secure and convenient access to digital services. A trustworthy identity system needs more than a working login screen: it must protect user credentials, verify identities correctly, secure communication, and handle personal information responsibly. This brief connects six areas that contribute to a secure digital identity service: OpenID Connect for authentication, AI risk management for suspicious-login detection, Android permissions, DNS over HTTPS, phishing-resistant authentication, and Oman’s personal data protection requirements. Together, these areas show how security, usability, privacy, and reliable system design depend on one another. The goal is to explain the main concepts, identify risks and misconceptions, and develop an informed question about designing a secure and user-friendly national digital login experience.
